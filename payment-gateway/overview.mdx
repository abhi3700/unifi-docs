The UniFi payment gateway is the integration surface for merchant and platform checkouts. It lets your product create a payment session, direct the payer to an authorization flow, and reconcile the resulting receipt.

## Recommended checkout flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the order">
    Generate your own immutable order ID and store the expected asset, network, recipient, amount, and expiry.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prepare the UniFi payment">
    Associate the payment attempt with your order on a trusted server and preflight the exact asset, network, amount, and recipient. Never trust an amount supplied only by the browser.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Present the payment">
    Show the payer the exact stablecoin-network pair, recipient, amount, fee treatment, and expiry.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize and execute">
    The payer connects a wallet, completes Permit2 approval if required, and signs the short-lived payment authorization.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reconcile">
    Store the receipt ID and transaction hash. Fulfil the order only after the receipt reaches the status required by your risk policy.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Integration rules

- Create and validate orders server-side.
- Make order IDs and payment attempts idempotent in your own system.
- Do not infer success from a browser redirect.
- Verify amount, asset, network, recipient, and receipt status.
- Handle `Failed` and `Reorged` explicitly.
- Keep API keys out of frontend code.
