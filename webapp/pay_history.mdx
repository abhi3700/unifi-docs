cards and loads older activity as you scroll.

address. Select a card to inspect the complete receipt.

## Find a payment

Select the search icon to open a focused search view. You can search using:

- stablecoin;
- network;
- amount;
- payer or recipient address;
- receipt ID.

History displays the number of matching payments and searches older activity when the currently
loaded results do not contain a match.

## Filter

The filter panel provides independent **Asset** and **Network** choices:

1. Choose a stablecoin, a network, or both.
2. Select **Show payments** to apply the selection.
3. Use the active-filter summary or **Clear** to remove the selection.

Leaving a category set to **All** keeps that part of the history unrestricted.

## Receipt cards

Selecting a payment card opens its receipt without losing your place in History. The receipt shows:

- the `Submitted`, `Confirmed`, and `Finalized` progression;
- the transferred asset, network, amount, and account address;
- fee savings when available;
- the blockchain reference and receipt ID;
- the payment date and share action.

## Empty state

An account with no payment activity sees **No Payments Yet!** with a **Pay** action. This is
different from a filtered empty state, which preserves the selected filters and offers
**Clear filters**.
