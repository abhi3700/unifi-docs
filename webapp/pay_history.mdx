---
title: "Payment history"
description: "Search and filter previous stablecoin payments."
icon: "clock-rotate-left"
---

Payment history lists receipts for the current user. Use it to:

- sort newest or oldest first
- filter by chain
- filter by status
- paginate through older or newer results
- open the full receipt and transaction hash

History is a view of recorded payment attempts, not only successful transfers. Check the status before using a row for reconciliation.
