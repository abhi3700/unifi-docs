---
title: "Deposit"
description: "Fund the correct wallet address with a supported stablecoin on the intended network."
icon: "arrow-down-to-line"
---

A deposit is a normal onchain transfer into the wallet address shown by UniFi.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the network">
    Select Ethereum, Polygon, or the network available in your environment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the address">
    Copy the displayed address or scan its QR code. Verify the first and last characters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the stablecoin">
    From the source wallet or exchange, select the same token and network shown in UniFi.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the balance">
    The balance appears after the network and UniFi have observed the transfer.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
Sending on the wrong network can make funds unavailable in the selected UniFi balance. Do a small test transfer when using a new source.
</Warning>
