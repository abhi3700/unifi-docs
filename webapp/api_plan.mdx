## Compare plans

The catalog exposes Monthly and Annual billing; Annual displays a 25% saving. Expand one tier at a
time or enable **Compare** to show every feature group.

| Tier | Included API credits | Request rate |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Free | 100k | 1 request/s |
| Starter | 1M | 10 requests/s |
| Growth | 20M | 20 requests/s |
| Scale | 50M | 30 requests/s |
| Enterprise | Custom | Custom |

Higher tiers include increasing bulk-payout, scheduled-payment, SLA, analytics, webhook, and
relayer allowances. Growth is marked Recommended. Enterprise uses a
**Talk to Us** action instead of self-service purchase.

## Purchase flow

Authenticated paid-plan selection proceeds through three visible stages:

```text
Plan selected → Payment → Confirmation
```

The payment stage changes from **Review & authorize** to **Submitted**, and Confirmation advances
from **Finality check** to **Waiting for finality** and then **Finalized**.

If a subscription payment is still pending, the page shows **Finalizing payment** and pauses plan
changes until blockchain confirmation is complete.

<Note>
Pricing and limits may change; the live catalog is the source of truth at purchase time.
</Note>
