---
title: "Balances"
description: "Read stablecoin balances by asset and network."
icon: "scale-balanced"
---

The wallet view groups value by stablecoin and shows the chain breakdown underneath it. A balance is tied to both dimensions:

- **Asset:** USDT, USDC, or DAI
- **Network:** Ethereum, Polygon, or an environment-specific test network

A displayed USD total is a convenience estimate based on the available price feed. Use the token balance—not the USD estimate—when validating whether a payment can be sent.

<Tip>
Run payment preflight before signing. It checks the spendable balance and estimated fee for the exact asset-network pair.
</Tip>
