A payment request packages the recipient and payment details into a shareable link or QR code.

## Create a request

1. Choose the stablecoin and network.
2. Enter the amount and an optional purpose.
3. Confirm the receiving address.
4. Generate the link or QR code.
5. Share it with the intended payer through a trusted channel.

The payer still reviews and signs the payment. A request cannot move funds by itself.

<Warning>
Treat payment links like invoices: verify the sender and the destination displayed in UniFi before authorizing.
</Warning>
