---
title: "Send a payment"
description: "Authorize a stablecoin transfer and track it to confirmation."
icon: "paper-plane"
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select the asset and chain">
    Choose an explicit pair such as **USDT on Ethereum**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter payment details">
    Provide the recipient address and amount. Add a short memo when useful.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review preflight">
    Check the available balance, estimated stablecoin fee, and whether a Permit2 approval is required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve once if needed">
    Approve the verified Permit2 contract for the selected token. Reusing the approval avoids another approval transaction for each later payment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign the payment">
    Review the recipient, amount, asset, network, and 15-minute expiry, then sign.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track the receipt">
    Keep the receipt ID and watch the payment move from `Processing` to `Confirmed` or `Finalized`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
The recipient receives the selected stablecoin on the selected network. Native gas shown as `0` applies to the supported payment step, not necessarily to a first-time token approval.
</Info>

See [Security and Permit2 permissions](/concepts/security-and-permissions) for the approval model.
