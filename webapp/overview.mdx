## Before you start

You need an EVM wallet, a supported stablecoin balance, and enough native gas for a first-time Permit2 approval when one is required. The subsequent supported payment flow does not require native gas from the payer.

<Note>
Always confirm the selected asset **and** network. “USDT on Ethereum” and “USDT on Polygon” are different payment routes.
</Note>
