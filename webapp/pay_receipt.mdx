A receipt is the durable view of a payment attempt. It records the selected asset and network,
amount, recipient, fee information, timestamps, receipt ID, and—after onchain submission—the
transaction hash and block reference.

## Status timeline

| Status | Meaning | What the receipt can show |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Submitted | Accepted into UniFi's execution flow and waiting for onchain confirmation | Request details and receipt ID; a transaction hash may not exist yet |
| Confirmed | Included in a block and waiting for the chain's finality threshold | Transaction hash, block reference, and paid timestamp |
| Finalized | Included in a finalized block; payment complete | Complete transfer and blockchain reference |
| Failed | Rejected before a valid onchain transfer completed | Failure state and retry guidance |
| Reorged | A previously confirmed block left the canonical chain | Reorg warning and retry guidance |

The main success progression is:

```text
Submitted → Confirmed → Finalized
```

Only the active status word is emphasized in the rendered timeline; the supporting copy remains at
normal weight.

## Receipt sections

- **Asset Transfer** — stablecoin, network, amount, destination, and fee details.
- **Blockchain Reference** — transaction hash and explorer action when one exists.
- **Receipt ID** — the UniFi identifier used by History, sharing, and receipt routes.

The page labels a processing receipt as **Submitted · Waiting for confirmation**, a confirmed
receipt as **Confirmed on-chain · Waiting for finality**, and the completed state as **Finalized
on-chain · Payment complete**.

## Refresh and share

Processing receipts expose a timed refresh action. The Share control uses the receipt capture root,
excludes interactive controls from the image, and passes the screenshot plus receipt URL to the
browser's Web Share path when supported.

<Warning>
Do not treat `Submitted` or `Confirmed` as irreversible settlement. Use `Finalized` for the
completed success state, and treat `Failed` and `Reorged` as unsuccessful terminal outcomes.
</Warning>
