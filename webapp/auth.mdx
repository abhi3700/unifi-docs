---
title: "Connect and authenticate"
description: "Use a wallet-signed challenge to establish a UniFi session."
icon: "lock"
---

UniFi uses wallet ownership to authenticate non-custodial users.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect your wallet">
    Select the wallet account you want UniFi to use. Check the address shown by the app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the challenge">
    UniFi issues a short-lived, single-use message containing the expected domain, URI, chain ID, nonce, and expiry.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign the message">
    Sign only when the wallet message matches the UniFi site and the account you selected. This is an authentication signature, not a token transfer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use the session">
    UniFi verifies the signature and creates a bearer session for protected requests.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
UniFi support will never ask for your seed phrase or private key.
</Warning>
