title: "Quickstart"
description: "Complete your first UniFi payment and verify its receipt."
---

This quickstart uses the hosted web app. You only need an EVM wallet and a supported stablecoin balance.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open UniFi">
    Open the [UniFi web app](https://unifiweb3.pages.dev/home) and connect your wallet. Confirm that the displayed address is the account you intend to use.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the asset and network">
    Select a supported pair such as **USDT on Ethereum** or **USDC on Polygon**. Asset availability can differ by environment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the payment">
    Choose **Send**, enter the recipient address and amount, then review the asset, network, recipient, and fee treatment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve Permit2 if needed">
    On the first payment for an asset, your wallet may ask you to approve Uniswap's Permit2 contract. This is an onchain token approval and can require native gas. Later payments can reuse that approval.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign the payment">
    Sign the UniFi payment authorization. This signature is scoped to the payment details and expires after 15 minutes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify the receipt">
    Save the receipt ID. Open the receipt page and wait for the status required by your workflow—usually `Confirmed` or `Finalized`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Before signing, verify the chain, stablecoin, recipient, amount, and expiry in your wallet. A signature should match the payment shown in UniFi.
</Tip>

## Build instead of click

If you are integrating UniFi into a product:

1. Read [Authentication](/api-reference/authentication).
2. Call the non-custodial preflight endpoint to fetch balance, estimated fee, and Permit2 allowance state.
3. Create the Permit2 EIP-712 signing data with a 15-minute deadline.
4. Ask the wallet to sign the returned typed data.
5. Submit the signature with the exact payment fields and store the returned receipt ID.
6. Poll the receipt endpoint until the payment reaches a terminal state.
