# Stablecoin payments without native gas

UniFi lets people send stablecoins while the execution layer handles the native network fee. The payer chooses an asset and network, reviews the payment, signs a short-lived authorization, and tracks the resulting onchain receipt.

## What UniFi handles

- **Gasless execution:** the payer does not need the chain's native token for each supported payment flow.
- **Stablecoin-first UX:** amounts, balances, fees, and receipts stay denominated in the selected stablecoin.
- **Permissioned signing:** non-custodial payments use Permit2 approval plus a payment authorization that expires after 15 minutes.
- **Receipt tracking:** submitted payments progress through `Processing`, `Confirmed`, `Finalized`, `Failed`, or `Reorged`.
- **Multiple interfaces:** use the web app, API, CLI, payment gateway, or Falcon.

<Note>
Production asset and network availability is environment-specific. The current service types include USDT, USDC, and DAI on Ethereum and Polygon, with Sepolia for testing and Anvil for local development.
</Note>
