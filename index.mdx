## What you can do

UniFi lets you send and request stablecoin payments using an address, link, or QR code. Make instant, low-cost transfers across chains without managing bridges, or send payments in bulk for payroll and batch payouts. Read more about [UniFi’s privacy-first payment design and goals](/mission).

- [Set up Unio](/webapp/unio#set-up-the-wallet) to pay with the self-custodial wallet built into UniFi.
- [Install UniFi](/webapp/install#install-unifi) to open the payment app from your home screen or desktop.
- [Send a payment](/webapp/pay_send) and review the asset, network, and amount before you authorize it.
- [Create a FliQ request](/webapp/pay_request) to share a payment link or QR code.
- [Use Falcon](/falcon/overview) to prepare payments through chat, schedule workflows, or read payment details from a snapshot.
- [Understand gasless payments and authorization](/concepts/security-and-permissions) before you approve a token or sign a payment.

<Warning>
    Verify whether a payment is `Confirmed` or `Finalized` before treating it as complete. [Understand confirmation and finality](/webapp/pay_receipt).
</Warning>

## Create API access

<Steps>
    <Step title="Open the Keys view">
        Sign in to the UniFi web app, open **API**, and select **New API Key**.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Create and secure the key">
        Give the key a unique integration name, create it, and copy it into a server-side secret manager.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Monitor or revoke access">
        Review credits from **Quota** and delete keys as soon as an integration no longer needs them.
    </Step>
</Steps>
